Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

OpenClaw Personal Assistant ("the app") is a private assistant used only by its owner, Low Weng Shan. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

How the data is used

  • Only to provide features the owner asks for: email summaries and reply drafts, calendar reminders, and reading or creating the owner's documents and spreadsheets.
  • Data is processed by the owner's own assistant server and the AI model provider (Google Gemini) to produce replies to the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than serving the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
