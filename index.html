OpenClaw Personal Assistant

This is a private, personal AI assistant that runs on WhatsApp for its owner, Low Weng Shan. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail to summarise important unread email and save reply drafts. It never sends email automatically.
  • Reads and adds events in the owner's Google Calendar and sends meeting reminders.
  • Finds, reads and creates files in the owner's Google Drive, Docs and Sheets (for example expenses, contacts and action items).

Google access is used only by the owner, for the owner's own account.
